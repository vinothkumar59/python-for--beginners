# Loops Interview Questions (Questions with Answers)

## 1. What is a loop?

**Answer:** A loop repeatedly executes a block of code.

---

## 2. Difference between for and while?

**Answer:**

- `for` is used when the number of iterations is known.
- `while` is used when iteration depends on a condition.

---

## 3. What is range()?

**Answer:** `range()` generates a sequence of numbers.

Example:

```python
range(1, 6)
```

---

## 4. What is break?

**Answer:** `break` immediately exits the loop.

---

## 5. What is continue?

**Answer:** `continue` skips the current iteration and moves to the next.

---

## 6. What is pass?

**Answer:** `pass` is a placeholder that performs no action.

---

## 7. What is enumerate()?

**Answer:** It returns both the index and value while iterating.

---

## 8. What is zip()?

**Answer:** It combines multiple iterables and iterates over them together.

---

## 9. What is a nested loop?

**Answer:** A loop inside another loop.

---

## 10. How are loops used in Data Engineering?

**Answer:** Loops are used for processing records, reading files, transforming data, validating rows, generating reports, and iterating through datasets.
